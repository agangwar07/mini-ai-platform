# Gas turbine T-17: maintenance standard (internal, rev 4)

Turbine T-17 is a 45 MW industrial gas turbine at the Riverside plant.

Inspection intervals are measured in equivalent operating hours (EOH):
- Borescope inspection of the hot gas path: every 8,000 EOH.
- Combustor inspection: every 25,000 EOH.
- Major overhaul: every 50,000 EOH.

Exhaust temperature limits: an alarm is raised at 610 °C and the unit trips at 630 °C.
A trip on exhaust temperature requires a borescope inspection before restart.
